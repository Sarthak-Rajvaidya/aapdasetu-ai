# Cyclone Preparedness and Coastal Safety

India's coastline is regularly affected by tropical cyclones, particularly
along the eastern (Bay of Bengal) and western (Arabian Sea) coasts.
Warnings are typically issued in stages with increasing urgency.

## Warning stages
- **Cyclone alert** (roughly 72 hours before expected landfall): begin
  preparations.
- **Cyclone warning** (roughly 24-48 hours before): finalize evacuation
  plans, especially if you live in a low-lying coastal area.
- **Post-landfall outlook**: continue following updates as the storm
  moves inland; heavy rain and flooding can continue well after landfall.

## Before landfall
- Secure loose outdoor items that could become projectiles in high wind.
- Charge devices and prepare an emergency kit, including drinking water
  and dry food.
- If you live in a low-lying coastal zone, plan to evacuate to a
  designated cyclone shelter or safe location inland well before landfall
  — storm surge can flood coastal areas rapidly.
- Keep important documents in a waterproof pouch.

## During the cyclone
- Stay indoors in a strong structure, away from windows and doors.
- Be aware of the "eye" of the cyclone: a temporary calm period during
  which winds may drop sharply before returning from the opposite
  direction with similar or greater force. Do not go outside during a
  lull unless officials confirm the storm has fully passed.
- Avoid coastal areas entirely due to storm surge risk, even if wind
  seems manageable inland.

## After the cyclone
- Wait for an official all-clear before returning to coastal or low-lying
  areas.
- Watch for flooding, debris, and downed power lines on the way home.
- Avoid contact with standing water, which may be contaminated or
  electrically live from downed lines.

This is general educational guidance and does not replace bulletins from
the India Meteorological Department (IMD) or local disaster management
authorities.
